Credential provider chain port (β.5).

CredentialProvider is the Strategy: each provider knows how to resolve a credential from one source (env, file, baked, …). CredentialChain is the composite that walks providers in order.

The port uses native async fn in trait (per ADR-0003); the dyn-compatible shim lives in [chain] and mirrors the boxed-future pattern from tau-runtime-core/src/builder.rs.
