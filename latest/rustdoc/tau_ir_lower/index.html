Std-side lowering for the tau workflow IR.

Holds the lower pass (tau_pkg::ProjectConfig → tau_ir::IrModule) and the LowerError type. Split out of tau-ir (β.7.5) so the pure IR crate and tau-runtime-core stay no_std and wasm-buildable.
