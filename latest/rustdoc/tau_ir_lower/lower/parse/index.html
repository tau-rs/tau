First lowering stage: pull typed declarations out of ProjectConfig.

This stage does no I/O and no resolution. It walks the ProjectConfig’s agent/tool/step tables and produces an in-memory Parsed value: a partially-populated Workflow whose ToolImpl::Native::content_hash and similar resolution slots are filled with zero bytes (the resolve stage fills them).
