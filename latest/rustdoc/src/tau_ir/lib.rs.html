6//! The tau workflow IR.
7//!
8//! See `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-31-workflow-ir-design.md` for the
9//! locked design. Per that spec:
10//!
11//! - Node types are typed full (Agent + Tool + Deterministic + Subflow) — see [`Node`].
12//! - The inter-node wire message is [`Message`] (a thin IR-owned mirror of
13//!   `tau_domain::Message`, with `SystemTime` normalized to `i64`-ms).
14//! - The IR is content-hashed; both `ir_format` and `tau_version` participate
15//!   in the hash. See `canonical` and `hash` modules.
