7//! See the constitution (G5) for why messages are the universal interaction primitive.

9//! `#![no_std]` + `alloc`: this is the runtime vocabulary the `no_std` wasm
10//! guest links through `tau-runtime-core`. The std-only corners (git
11//! `PackageSource` URL parsing, SKILL.md YAML) live behind the host-only
12//! `package-source` / `skill-md` features.
